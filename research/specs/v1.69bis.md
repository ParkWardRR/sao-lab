# Shitty Add-On v1.69bis

- **Source:** <https://hackaday.com/2019/03/20/introducing-the-shitty-add-on-v1-69bis-standard/> (Brian Benchoff, Hackaday, 2019-03-20)
- **Fetched:** 2026-11-09. The numbers below are quoted from the article; this closes the "unverified" flags in [electrical-and-interop.md](electrical-and-interop.md).
- **Linked spec pages (not fetched by us):** [hackaday.io SAO project 52950](https://hackaday.io/project/52950-shitty-add-ons), [v1.69bis announcement log](https://hackaday.io/project/52950/log/159806-introducing-the-shitty-add-on-v169bis-standard), [ANDnXOR reference designs](https://github.com/ANDnXOR/sao-reference-designs), [MiniBadge footprint](https://github.com/lukejenkins/minibadge/blob/master/minibadge-footprint.png)

The article itself says it is **"not the official, final release"**; a few things were "still being worked out, especially concerning the function of the two additional pins". Treat it as informal.

## Rules, quoted

| Topic | Article text |
|---|---|
| Signal voltage | "No signals may exceed 3.6V." |
| GPIO drive | "If a Shitty Add-on drives a GPIO pin, it MUST be through a resistor of at least 330 ohms." Says nothing about input-only use. |
| Power | "Maximum power draw by a Shitty Add-On is 1.1 millihorsepower." No W or mA figure in the article. |
| Power-only SAO | "Utilize only VCC (3v3) and GND to do whatever you want. Other pins should be NC." |
| Badge connector | "2x3 female (non pinned) headers, 0.1" pitch." Through-hole highly suggested "lest SAOs rip off your pads". May be keyed, but keyed through-hole 2x3 parts are "either impossible to find or absurdly expensive". |
| SAO connector | "2x3 male, pinned, shrouded connectors"; through-hole header recommended for mechanical strength. |

**Correction to earlier notes:** the 0.82 W / ~250 mA at 3.3 V conversion is **not in the article**. It comes from reader comments. 1.1 mhp is 0.82 W by arithmetic (1 hp = 745.7 W), which gives about 250 mA at 3.3 V, but that is our derivation. Use it as a ceiling estimate only.

The pinout in the article is an image; the text gives no pin positions. It is "exactly the same as last year's version, with the addition of two GPIO pins". v1 was 3.3 V, GND, SDA, SCL on a 0.1" grid.

## GPIO1 / GPIO2 conventions

- **WS2812 / NeoPixel:** the badge either connects the **end** of its existing WS2812 strand's data line to GPIO1, or drives GPIO1 from a badge GPIO with a stream meant for the SAO. Remember to add about 10 to the strand length.
- **APA102 / DotStar:** GPIO1 is data (DO / MISO), GPIO2 is clock (CO / CLK). If driven at 5 V a divider on the badge side may bring it to 3.3 V; a direct connection is fine "provided that the pin never exceeds 3.3V".
- **SK9822:** "basically the same as APA102".
- **UART (discouraged):** GPIO1 = badge TX / SAO RX; GPIO2 = badge RX / SAO TX; "A default baud rate of 115200 is recommended". The SAO must not send on GPIO2 until it has received input from the badge. The article says serial on an SAO is "generally a bad idea" and "will require working with a badge maker".

## Unfinished in the article

- **No identification.** "There's no way to tell if a Shitty Add-on supports WS2812, APA102, or serial connections." A voltage divider on GPIO1 and an I2C flash chip were floated; neither specified ("We have no idea how this would work yet"). A reference I2C EEPROM SAO exists, "identical to the Tide Pod SAO of last year".
- A mechanical standard (zip-tie hole or M3 bolt) was discussed, not adopted.
